## Essential cookies and storage

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|------|---------|----------|
| `ra.session_token` (on HTTPS: `__Secure-ra.session_token`) | Keeps you signed in; protected with HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite | 7 days, renewed while you use the site |
| `ra.dont_remember` | Ends the sign-in when the browser closes, if you chose not to stay signed in | Browser session |
| `ra.two_factor` | Completes the two-factor sign-in step | A few minutes |
| `ra_locale` | Remembers your language | 1 year |
| `ra_consent` | Remembers your cookie choices | 6 months |
| `ra_a11y` (browser storage, not a cookie) | Remembers reading aids you chose in the accessibility menu; stays on your device | Until you reset it |
